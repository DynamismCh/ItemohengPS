Who we are

A school community with a shared purpose.

Itemoheng Primary School is a school where stakeholders are committed to helping learners and educators achieve their academic goals.

Our vision

To see all stakeholders display maximum commitment in assisting learners and educators to attain academic goals.

Our mission

To ensure educators and learners spend quality time during curriculum delivery, leading to excellent results through effective teaching and learning. We continuously seek parental support, deploy resources to support teaching and learning, and work to ensure every learner leaves the institution fully matured and educated.
